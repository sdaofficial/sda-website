Become a Member of SDA

To join the Sustainable Development Advocate University of Lagos, applicants must demonstrate a commitment to sustainable development principles. Basic requirements include being a student of the University of Lagos , and completion of the online application form. Applicants are expected to undergo an interim class which upon completion would submit essays on various topics outlining their passion for sustainable development and their goals within the field. Successful candidates will be selected based on their participation during the classes and alignment with the SDA's mission as communicated in their submitted essays.
