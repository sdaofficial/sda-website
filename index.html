Our history

Uniting Youth for Sustainable Development with SDA.

We are an organization dedicated to raising awareness for the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). While we leverage global expertise to shape our organizational framework, our projects are implemented with a local focus, emphasizing direct engagement with both rural and urban communities. As a dynamic force dedicated to driving positive impact, SDA proudly leads the charge in youth-led initiatives, placing the banner of sustainable development at the forefront of our endeavors.

“

At our core lies a mission that pulses with purpose — to illuminate the path towards Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and actively contribute to their realization. We champion the potency of youth inclusiveness, capacity building, and the cultivation of job innovators poised to steer progress across the 5 P's of SDGs: People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace, and Partnership.

“

SDA Goals

To create awareness about the Sustainable Development Goals. Advancing the SDGs through youth inclusivity, sensitization and implementation of social projects. To ensure that no one is left behind on the 5 P's of SDGs: People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace and Partnership.

A Message From the Staff Adviser

Prof James Akanmu, PhD, FNSE

Staff Adviser Sustainable Development Advocates

My Dear Sustainable Development Advocates,
I am Professor James Akanmu, PhD, FNSE, and I am honored to serve as the Staff Adviser to this esteemed organization. SDA plays a vital role in our university community, aligning itself with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to drive positive change both on campus and beyond. SDA offers its members a way to contribute to society, our diverse teams tackle various SDGs through impactful projects- (a link to the club activities section). These projects inspire and empower individuals and communities to make informed decisions that contribute to tangible progress towards achieving the SDGs. I encourage each member to actively participate in the organization and not waste the opportunities provided so we can all work to the shared vision of a more sustainable world.

This webpage serves as a central hub for information, resources, and updates on our activities. Here, you will find opportunities to get involved, learn more about sustainability issues, and connect with like-minded individuals who share your passion for creating a better world.
Explore our webpage, engage with our content, and join us in our mission to champion sustainability in all its forms. Remember, active participation can be balanced with your academic pursuits. Together, we can make a real difference on both the University campus and beyond.
